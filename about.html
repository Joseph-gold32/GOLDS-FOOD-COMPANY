How It Started

From Family Kitchen
to Gold Standard

Gold Foods was born in 2020 from a simple belief: everyone deserves a truly exceptional meal. What started as home cooking for friends and family quickly became Lagos' most talked-about food delivery service.

Our founder, a passionate self-taught chef, spent years studying culinary traditions across the world before bringing those flavours home to Nigeria. Today, our team of professional chefs crafts every dish with the same love and attention that started it all.
